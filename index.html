ABOUT US

Building With Purpose

Dar Sameem Contracting is a Saudi contracting company delivering integrated construction and technical solutions across a wide range of projects.

Our expertise covers fit-out and finishing works, MEP works, data and low current systems, infrastructure, and building works, with a strong focus on quality, coordination, and timely delivery.

Our recent projects include combat halls for the Saudi Olympic & Paralympic Committee in Riyadh and Jeddah.
